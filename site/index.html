Questions

What does the command do?

It downloads the GPS Spoofer Installer app from this site into your Applications folder and opens it. Nothing else runs until you press Install. All of the code is open on GitHub.

Why does it need Xcode?

Xcode is Apple's free tool for putting apps on your own iPhone. The installer uses it to build GPS Spoofer and sign it with your Apple ID, so no jailbreak is needed. It's a large download, which is most of the hour.

Does it work with a free Apple ID?

Yes. Apple makes apps signed with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days. When that happens, plug in your iPhone and open the installer again; it renews in a couple of minutes. A paid Apple Developer account makes it last a year.

Do I need my Mac after installing?

No. Once it's set up, GPS Spoofer works on its own. Install the free LocalDevVPN app from the App Store and keep it connected while you use GPS Spoofer.
